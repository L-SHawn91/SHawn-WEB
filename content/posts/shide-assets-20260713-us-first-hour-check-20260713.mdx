## Three Things to Know

### 1. Semiconductors Took the Hardest Hit — but Stabilized Quickly

SOXX gapped down -3.12% at the open and SMH -2.33%, far worse than the broader QQQ (-1.06%). However, the first hour showed no acceleration: SOXX recovered +0.26%, AMD +1.36%, and Micron +1.57% intraday. This suggests the selling was concentrated in the opening gap, with limited follow-through.

### 2. Not a Panic — Gold Fell, Bonds Were Calm

Key panic indicators are absent. Gold dropped -2.44% (not being bought as a safe haven). The US 10-year yield barely moved (+0.024pp). The VIX at 16.29 is elevated but far below the 20+ threshold that signals systemic stress. The Dow (-0.05%) and S&P 500 (-0.28%) were barely affected.

### 3. Oil Surge Adds a Separate Pressure Layer

WTI crude jumped +4.62% to $74.71, driven by US-Iran tensions. This is a separate risk channel from the semiconductor weakness, but it compounds the cautious mood.

## What This Means for Korea (July 14)

Today’s KOSPI -8.95% circuit-breaker event is reverberating through global markets. With US semiconductors soft but not crashing, the Korean market is likely to open lower but not face another extreme rout. Key points:

- KOSPI: Gap-down opening of -2% to -3%, then range-bound near the 6,600 level.

- Semiconductor names: Under pressure from SOXX/SMH weakness. Micron’s -4.24% adds specific concern for memory/HBM names.

- Won strength (₩1,494.88): A modest positive signal — foreign capital outflow pressure may be easing.
