## Morning Pulse: Pre-Holiday Semiconductor Pressure

The KOSPI opened at 6,914.79 (-5.07%) and continued lower to 6,823.87 (-6.32%) as of 09:49 KST. The KOSDAQ is at 797.62 (-3.83%), showing relative resilience among smaller-cap names.

## What’s Driving the Market

1. **Pre-holiday position reduction**: With Korea’s Constitution Day (July 17) closing markets tomorrow, institutional investors are reducing exposure ahead of the 3-day weekend.

2. **US semiconductor profit-taking spillover**: The previous US session saw SOXX -2.23% and MU -8.02%. This semiconductor weakness is transmitting to Korean chip giants with amplified intensity — a pattern observed across the current cycle.

3. **Mega-cap concentration, not broad weakness**: The rise/fall ratio shows more advancing stocks than declining ones. The KOSPI’s -6.32% is being driven by the extreme weight of a few large-cap semiconductor names, while mid/small-caps are holding up better.

4. **Global backdrop remains stable**: VIX at 15.67, S&P 500 futures near flat, and stable KRW/USD suggest no systemic global shock. This is a local Korea positioning event ahead of a holiday.
