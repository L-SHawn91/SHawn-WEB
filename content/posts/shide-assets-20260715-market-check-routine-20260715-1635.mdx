## Today’s Market: Crash Recovery Rally Confirmed

July 15 KOSPI closed at **7,284.41 (+6.24%)**, completing a four-day crash-exhaustion recovery cycle. The July 13 rout (-8.95%) has been fully reversed in two sessions: Monday’s V-shaped rebound (+5.53%) followed by today’s gap-up sustained rally. KOSDAQ closed at 829.43 (+5.80%), confirming broad participation.

The rally was powered by overseas semiconductor strength (US SOXX +2.58%, NVDA +4.06%) and institutional buying: foreign net purchases reached +23.2 billion KRW, with program buying at +16.7 billion KRW — structural inflows that intensified through the afternoon rather than fading.

## What Changed

- Crash cycle terminated : Four consecutive constructive sessions confirm the July 13 crash was a single tail event, not the start of a systemic decline. Failed-recovery probability: ~0%.

- Structural buying pattern : Unlike typical midday erosion, foreign and program flows accelerated between 12:00–15:30, neutralizing profit-taking pressure at the index level.

- Mega-cap semis saw concentrated profit-taking : While the KOSPI held most gains, individual large-cap semiconductor names gave back 1–3% from intraday highs — normal after single-session moves exceeding +10%.

- Won stability : USD/KRW held near 1,488, supporting the foreign-inflow thesis.
