✅ Crash Cycle Ending — Four-Way Macro Confirmation

The combination of VIX declining below 20, US Treasury yields easing, the Dollar weakening, and the Korean Won strengthening to 1,488 creates a constructive macro environment. US semiconductors are recovering broadly (SOXX +2.4%, AMD +3.5%, MU +3.3%), confirming that Monday’s decline was a liquidity event rather than a structural breakdown. The pre-market fade in semiconductors is a cautionary data point but does not invalidate the positive direction — it reflects the well-documented gap between low-liquidity pre-market pricing and regular-session execution.
