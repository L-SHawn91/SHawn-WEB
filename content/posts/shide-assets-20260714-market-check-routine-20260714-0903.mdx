## Key Signals

- Crash exhaustion confirmed: After Monday’s -8.95% KOSPI decline, additional selling pressure is minimal. The market had already priced in the worst-case scenario.

- Samsung leads recovery: +2% on institutional dip-buying — a strong signal that crash-day lows are being tested and held.

- Won stability is the anchor: USD/KRW at 1,497, unchanged from yesterday. This is preventing foreign investor flight.

- Oil pressure contained: WTI crude at $79 but KRX impact is limited — Korea’s market sensitivity to oil is lower than US.

- Inverse ETF falling: KODEX Inverse -0.45% — the market is not betting on further collapse.
