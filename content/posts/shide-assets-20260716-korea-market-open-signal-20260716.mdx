## Market Snapshot

**Thursday, July 16, 2026 — 10:28 KST**

Korea’s KOSPI opened under significant pressure this morning, driven by pre-holiday positioning and concentrated selling in large-cap semiconductor names. The index is trading approximately 7% below the previous session’s close, with semiconductor stocks accounting for most of the decline. However, mid-cap and smaller names are showing relative resilience, with some recovering from early-session lows — indicating a divergence between the headline index and the broader market.

## Key Observations

- Semiconductor pressure : Large-cap chip makers are facing concentrated selling, echoing last night’s US semiconductor weakness. Memory chip stocks in particular are under heavy pressure.

- Market-wide divergence : While the KOSPI headline index shows significant decline, the number of rising stocks exceeds declining stocks — small and mid-cap names are holding up better.

- External environment stable : US futures are flat, the VIX volatility index remains at 15.67 (indicating no panic), and the Korean won is steady against the dollar. There is no external systemic shock driving this session.

- Pre-holiday factor : Friday July 17 is Constitution Day, a Korean market holiday. The three-day weekend is amplifying profit-taking ahead of the break.

## What to Watch

- Whether large-cap semis stabilize or continue to erode through midday

- If the KOSPI can reclaim the 6,800 level, indicating bargain-hunting interest

- Mid-cap recovery signals — if strength broadens beyond a few names

- The next regular session after the holiday: Monday, July 20
