## What Happened

Korean markets experienced their worst single-day decline in years, with KOSPI falling 8.95%. The selloff was triggered by a confluence of shocks:

1. **Crude oil surge (+4.11%)**: WTI spiked above $74 on supply concerns, fueling inflation fears and hitting oil-sensitive sectors.
2. **Semiconductor crash**: SK Hynix plunged 15.37%, approaching individual circuit-breaker levels. Samsung Electronics fell 10.70%.
3. **Failed recovery pattern**: An early V-shaped recovery (~09:25 KST) reversed into a full rout by midday, triggering mechanical selling from leveraged products and program trading.
4. **Won weakness**: USD/KRW rose above 1,503, adding pressure from foreign investor outflows.
5. **US futures weakness**: Nasdaq futures declined ~1.4% during Asian hours, compounding tech sector concerns.

The KOSDAQ, which initially gained +2.7% on rotation into small caps, reversed to close -4.55% as panic spread across all sectors.

## Key Signal: Extreme Oversold

- KOSPI -8.95% is at levels last seen during the 2008 financial crisis and 2020 COVID shock.
- SK Hynix at 1,845,000 (-15.37%) is near individual circuit-breaker territory.
- KODEX Leverage ETF fell 19.11%, reflecting forced liquidation of leveraged positions.
- The afternoon continued to deteriorate rather than consolidate — a sign that selling pressure was mechanical rather than fundamentally driven.

This type of extreme selloff historically produces:
- **Short-term**: Technical bounces of 2-5% within 1-3 sessions are common after -8%+ drops.
